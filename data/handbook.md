# Drini Logistics sh.p.k. Employee Handbook

Version 2026.1. Applies to all employees of Drini Logistics sh.p.k., Rruga e Kavajes 112, Tirana. This is a fictional company used for a demonstration.

## 1. About this handbook
1.1 This handbook explains how we work at Drini Logistics. It applies to all roughly 60 employees in the Tirana office, the Porto Romano warehouse and the driver team.
1.2 Where the Albanian Labour Code gives you more than this handbook, the Labour Code applies. Where this handbook gives you more, this handbook applies.
1.3 HR can be reached at hr@drini-logistics.example or on internal line 210. The HR manager is Elira Hoxha. Questions about this handbook should go to her first.

## 2. Working hours
2.1 The standard working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, 08:30 to 17:00 with a 30 minute unpaid lunch break.
2.2 Warehouse staff work in two shifts: early 06:00 to 14:30 and late 13:30 to 22:00. Shift rosters are published every Thursday for the following week.
2.3 Office staff may start between 08:00 and 09:30 if they agree it with their manager, as long as they complete 8 hours. Core hours, when everyone must be reachable, are 10:00 to 15:30.

## 3. Public holidays
3.1 Employees do not work on the public holidays set by Albanian law, including 1 January, 14 March (Summer Day), 22 March (Nowruz), 1 May, 28 November and 25 December. Muslim, Catholic and Orthodox religious holidays follow the official calendar.
3.2 Holidays that fall on a weekend are not moved to Monday unless the law says so.
3.3 Warehouse and driver staff who are asked to work on a public holiday are paid 100% extra for the hours worked, see section 18 on overtime, and may not be forced to accept.

## 4. Annual leave entitlement
4.1 Every employee receives 26 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees with more than 5 years of service receive 28 working days, and employees with more than 10 years receive 30 working days.
4.2 During the first year, leave builds up at 2 days per full month worked. Public holidays and sick days do not count as annual leave.
4.3 Leave is paid at your normal basic salary.

## 5. Requesting annual leave
5.1 Request leave in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the first day of leave. Requests for 1 to 2 days need only 3 working days notice.
5.2 Your manager answers within 3 working days. If there is no answer after 3 working days, ask HR.
5.3 Leave in July and August is limited to 15 consecutive working days so that each team keeps cover. In December the warehouse may refuse leave during the peak week of 15 to 24 December.

## 6. Carrying over leave
6.1 Unused annual leave can be carried over to the next year up to a maximum of 5 working days. Carried over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire without payment.
6.2 Leave that is not used and not carried over is lost, unless the company refused your request, in which case HR will agree a new date with you.
6.3 When employment ends, unused leave for the current year is paid out together with your final salary, see section 17.

## 7. Sick leave: what to do when you are ill
7.1 If you cannot come to work because of illness, call or message your manager before the start of your shift, and in any case by 09:00 on the first day. Do not send the message through a colleague.
7.2 For absences of 1 to 2 days no doctor's note is needed. From the third consecutive day you must bring a medical certificate from a doctor.
7.3 Sick days are not taken from annual leave. If you fall ill during annual leave and have a medical certificate, the sick days are returned to your leave balance.

## 8. Sick leave: pay
8.1 For the first 14 calendar days of an illness, the company pays 80% of your normal salary. From day 15, the Social Insurance Institute pays the sickness benefit, and the company pays nothing.
8.2 The company pays 100% of salary for absences caused by a workplace accident, see section 40.
8.3 Pregnancy related check-ups are not counted as sick days. You may take the time with 2 working days notice and a copy of the appointment.

## 9. Long-term illness and return to work
9.1 After an absence of more than 30 calendar days you will have a return to work conversation with HR and your manager, to agree any adjustments such as lighter duties or shorter days.
9.2 Your job is protected while you receive sickness benefit from the Social Insurance Institute.
9.3 The company can ask you to see an occupational doctor, at the company's cost, if there is a safety concern about your return.

## 10. Maternity leave
10.1 Maternity leave is 365 calendar days. 35 days are taken before the birth and 70 days must be taken after the birth.
10.2 For the first 150 days the Social Insurance Institute pays 80% of your salary. For days 151 to 365 it pays 50%. The company does not top this up, except for the first month after birth where it tops it up to 100%.
10.3 Tell HR in writing at least 6 weeks before your planned start date and bring the doctor's certificate confirming the expected birth date.

## 11. Paternity and adoption leave
11.1 Fathers have 5 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 30 days of the birth. It is paid by the company at 100% of salary.
11.2 Employees who adopt a child under 5 years old have the same entitlement as for maternity leave in section 10, shared between the parents as they choose.
11.3 Ask for paternity leave in the HR portal with the birth certificate, as soon as it is issued.

## 12. Bereavement and family leave
12.1 You receive 3 working days of paid leave on the death of a spouse, child, parent or sibling, and 1 working day for a grandparent, parent-in-law or other close relative.
12.2 Leave for your own wedding is 5 working days paid, and 1 day for the wedding of a child. Give 10 working days notice.
12.3 Bereavement and wedding leave is separate from annual leave and is not deducted from it.

## 13. Unpaid leave
13.1 You can request up to 30 calendar days of unpaid leave per year for personal reasons, once you have finished probation.
13.2 Longer unpaid leave, up to 6 months, needs written approval from the managing director.
13.3 During unpaid leave you do not receive salary and annual leave does not build up. The company keeps paying social insurance only if you ask and cover the contribution.

## 14. Probation
14.1 New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. For managers and specialist roles it is 6 months.
14.2 During probation either side can end the employment with 7 calendar days written notice, with no reason needed.
14.3 You have a review meeting with your manager at the end of month 1 and at the end of probation. At the end of probation HR confirms in writing whether you are permanent.
14.4 Annual leave is earned during probation but may only be taken for emergencies in the first 3 months.

## 15. Notice period when you resign
15.1 If you want to leave after probation, give written notice. The notice period is 30 calendar days if you have worked less than 2 years, 60 calendar days for 2 to 5 years, and 90 calendar days for more than 5 years.
15.2 Managers and team leads give 90 calendar days notice regardless of length of service.
15.3 You may agree a shorter notice period with the managing director in writing. During notice you keep your salary and must take any remaining leave only if the company agrees.

## 16. Notice period when the company ends your contract
16.1 If the company ends your contract for reasons that are not disciplinary, we give you the same notice as in section 15, and at least 30 calendar days.
16.2 If your role is made redundant, you receive the notice period plus a severance payment of one month of salary for each full year of service, up to a maximum of 6 months.
16.3 Dismissal for serious misconduct under section 38 can be immediate and without notice or severance.

## 17. Final pay and returning property
17.1 Your final salary, including unused leave, is paid on the next regular pay date after your last day, and no later than 10 working days after.
17.2 On or before your last day return your laptop, badge, keys, fuel card and any other company property to HR. The IT team will wipe your accounts the same day.
17.3 The company may deduct the value of unreturned equipment from your final pay only with your written agreement.

## 18. Overtime
18.1 Overtime must be approved by your manager before it is worked. Unapproved overtime is not paid.
18.2 Approved overtime on a normal working day is paid at 125% of your hourly rate. Overtime on a Saturday or Sunday is paid at 150%, and on a public holiday at 200%.
18.3 Overtime may not exceed 2 hours per day or 200 hours per year per person. Instead of pay you may ask for time off at the same rate, to be used within 3 months.

## 19. Night shifts and weekend work
19.1 Night work between 22:00 and 06:00 is paid at 130% of your hourly rate, in addition to your normal pay.
19.2 A driver or warehouse employee who works at least 4 hours on a Sunday receives an allowance of 3,000 ALL, as an addition to the overtime rate in section 18 if it applies.
19.3 No employee may work more than 6 days in a row. There must be at least 11 hours of rest between two shifts.

## 20. Working from home: who can do it
20.1 Office staff in finance, customer service, dispatch planning and HR can work from home up to 2 days per week, after probation.
20.2 Warehouse and driver roles cannot work from home because their work is on site.
20.3 Your manager can ask you to come to the office on a remote day with 24 hours notice for team meetings or client visits. Agree your remote days in the HR portal at the start of each quarter.

## 21. Working from home: equipment and costs
21.1 Remote workers get a monthly home office allowance of 2,500 ALL, paid with salary, to cover internet and electricity. No receipts are needed.
21.2 The company does not pay for furniture or a second internet line. A monitor, keyboard and mouse can be borrowed from IT for your remote days, see section 22.
21.3 You must use the company VPN on any home or public network, and may not let other people in your home use company devices.

## 22. Company laptops and equipment
22.1 Office employees receive a company laptop, and a replacement after 4 years of use. Request equipment from IT through the helpdesk portal. IT delivers within 5 working days.
22.2 Company equipment is for work. Personal use must be small, and you may not install software that IT has not approved or let family members use the device.
22.3 If equipment is lost or stolen, tell IT within 24 hours. If it was lost through serious negligence, the company can ask you to pay up to 50% of its current value, with your written agreement.

## 23. Mobile phones and SIM cards
23.1 Drivers, dispatchers and managers receive a company mobile phone and SIM with a monthly data and call plan. The company pays up to 3,000 ALL per month.
23.2 If your personal use pushes the bill above 3,000 ALL, you pay the difference, deducted with your written consent.
23.3 Other employees can use their own phone for work if they want to, but do not get a plan, and the company pays 1,000 ALL per month if they use the work mobile app for dispatch.

## 24. Company vehicles and fuel cards
24.1 Company vans and trucks are only for company journeys and must be driven only by employees who have the right licence category, which HR checks every year.
24.2 Fuel cards may only be used to fuel the vehicle they are assigned to. Keep the receipt and log the kilometres at every fill-up.
24.3 Traffic fines are paid by the driver. The company pays parking fines only for a delivery stop that was approved in dispatch.
24.4 Private use of company vehicles, including taking one home, needs a signed approval from the operations director.

## 25. Business travel approval
25.1 All business trips outside Tirana need approval from your manager in the HR portal at least 5 working days before departure. Trips abroad need approval from the managing director, 10 working days ahead.
25.2 Book flights and hotels through the office manager, who uses our agency. Economy class is standard, and hotels are limited to 100 EUR per night in the EU and 80 EUR in the Balkans.
25.3 Cancel through the office manager immediately if plans change.

## 26. Travel per diems
26.1 For a business trip within Albania with an overnight stay, you receive a daily allowance of 3,500 ALL.
26.2 For trips abroad the daily allowance is 45 EUR in the Balkans, 60 EUR in the rest of Europe and 75 EUR elsewhere, as paid in the currency of the trip.
26.3 On the first and the last day of a trip you receive half the amount. If the client or a hotel provides breakfast, lunch or dinner, the allowance is reduced by 20% for each meal provided.
26.4 Per diems are paid in advance with salary or in cash from the cashier two working days before departure. No receipts are needed for the allowance itself.

## 27. Expense claims
27.1 For anything other than the per diem, such as taxis, parking, tolls, train tickets and small tools, submit an expense claim in the HR portal within 30 calendar days of spending the money. Claims submitted later will not be paid.
27.2 Attach the original receipt or invoice for every item. For items without a fiscal receipt, add a written statement approved by your manager, with a limit of 2,000 ALL per month.
27.3 Approved claims are reimbursed in the next monthly salary. Claims above 30,000 ALL need approval from the finance director.
27.4 You may not claim for personal items, fines or alcohol.

## 28. Meals and client entertainment
28.1 A business meal with a client or supplier may be claimed up to 3,000 ALL per person, with the receipt and the names of those attending.
28.2 Meals are not claimable when you are travelling and receive a per diem, see section 26, unless you are hosting a client.
28.3 Team lunches need prior approval from the department head and are limited to 1,500 ALL per person, twice a year.

## 29. Salary payment and payslips
29.1 Salary is paid by bank transfer on the 5th of the following month. If the 5th is a weekend or a holiday, it is paid on the previous working day.
29.2 Your payslip is in the HR portal on the day of payment. Tell finance within 10 working days if you think there is a mistake.
29.3 The 13th month bonus is not paid. A performance bonus may be paid in December as described in section 31.

## 30. Salary advances and loans
30.1 After probation you may ask for a salary advance of up to 50% of your net monthly salary. Only one advance may be outstanding at a time.
30.2 The advance is repaid in full from your next salary. There is no interest or fee.
30.3 The company does not provide loans. In an emergency, such as a medical event, the managing director may agree an exceptional advance of up to 2 monthly salaries, repaid over at most 6 months.

## 31. Performance reviews and pay rises
31.1 Every employee has a performance review in January with their manager. Goals for the year are set in the same meeting and rated at a mid-year check in July.
31.2 Salary rises are decided in February after the reviews, and take effect from 1 March.
31.3 A performance bonus of up to one month of salary may be paid in December if the company meets its yearly target, and depends on your rating.

## 32. Training and certifications
32.1 Each employee has a training budget of 40,000 ALL per year for courses, conferences and exams that are relevant to the job. Agree it with your manager before you book.
32.2 Forklift, ADR dangerous goods and tachograph certifications are paid by the company in full and do not count against the budget.
32.3 If you leave within 12 months after a course costing more than 50,000 ALL, you repay a share of the cost in proportion to the months missing, as agreed in a written training agreement.

## 33. Personal data protection
33.1 We process personal data of employees, drivers and customer contacts according to Albanian Law 124/2024 on personal data and the GDPR where it applies. Only access data that you need for your job.
33.2 Never send lists of customers or employees to a personal email address, messaging app or cloud account.
33.3 If you think personal data has been exposed, lost or sent to the wrong person, report it to the Data Protection Officer at dpo@drini-logistics.example within 24 hours. The company must tell the regulator within 72 hours when required.
33.4 You can ask to see, correct or delete the data that we keep about you by writing to HR. We reply within 30 days.

## 34. Information security and passwords
34.1 Passwords must have at least 12 characters and be changed only if you suspect they were exposed. Use the company password manager and never share a password, even with IT.
34.2 Multi-factor authentication is required on email, the HR portal and the dispatch system.
34.3 Lock your screen when you leave your desk. Do not plug unknown USB sticks into company laptops, and report suspicious emails to security@drini-logistics.example using the report button.

## 35. Confidentiality of cargo and customer information
35.1 Information about customers, cargo contents, routes and prices is confidential, during and for 2 years after employment.
35.2 Do not discuss shipments, customs documents or customers with anyone outside the company, including family and friends.
35.3 If a customer, journalist or authority asks for information, refer them to the managing director.

## 36. Harassment and discrimination: how to report
36.1 Drini Logistics does not tolerate harassment, bullying, sexual harassment or discrimination based on gender, ethnicity, religion, age, disability or any other protected reason.
36.2 You can report to your manager, to HR, or directly to the Ethics Contact, Mirela Prifti, at ethics@drini-logistics.example. If the report concerns your manager or HR, go directly to the Ethics Contact.
36.3 We acknowledge your report within 2 working days and start an investigation within 5 working days. You will be told the outcome, in general terms, within 30 calendar days.
36.4 Nobody who reports in good faith will be punished for it. Retaliation is itself serious misconduct.

## 37. Reporting wrongdoing (whistleblowing)
37.1 If you see fraud, theft, bribery, customs violations or dangerous practices, you can report them confidentially through the anonymous form on the intranet or to the Ethics Contact. This is different from a personal complaint about how you are treated, which is in section 36.
37.2 Reports can be anonymous. We confirm receipt within 7 calendar days when you leave a contact method and give feedback within 3 months.
37.3 You are protected from dismissal or other disadvantage for a good faith report, in line with Albanian whistleblower law.

## 38. Disciplinary procedure
38.1 For minor breaches, such as repeated lateness, the manager gives a verbal warning, then a written warning, then a final written warning.
38.2 Before any written warning you have the right to explain your side in a meeting, and to bring a colleague or a union representative with you.
38.3 Serious misconduct, such as theft, violence, working under the influence of alcohol, falsifying delivery documents or serious breaches of data protection, may lead to dismissal without notice.
38.4 A written warning stays on your file for 12 months.

## 39. Health, safety and protective equipment
39.1 Warehouse staff must wear safety shoes, a high-visibility vest and gloves when handling goods. The company provides this equipment free of charge and replaces it when worn out.
39.2 Only trained and certified employees may drive forklifts or reach trucks. Maximum manual lifting is 25 kg per person; use a trolley or ask for help above this.
39.3 Smoking is allowed only in the marked area behind building B. Fire drills are held twice a year and everyone must take part.

## 40. Accidents and incident reporting
40.1 Report every accident, injury and near miss to your manager and to the safety officer on the same day, even if nothing seems serious. Use the form in the safety folder or call internal line 199.
40.2 In an emergency call 127 for an ambulance first. The first aid boxes are by every exit and in each vehicle.
40.3 An accident on the way to or from work or during work is a workplace accident. Salary is paid at 100% as stated in section 8.

## 41. Dress code
41.1 Office staff dress smart casual. Clients visiting the office may be present, so avoid ripped clothes and sportswear.
41.2 Drivers and warehouse staff wear the company uniform, two sets of which are given on joining and replaced once a year.
41.3 On Fridays, office staff may dress casual, unless they have client meetings.

## 42. Conflicts of interest and gifts
42.1 You must tell your manager about any outside work, business or family relationship with a supplier, customer or competitor that could affect your decisions.
42.2 You may accept gifts from suppliers or customers up to a value of 5,000 ALL. Anything above must be declined, or handed to HR. Never accept cash.
42.3 Second jobs are allowed outside working hours if they do not compete with Drini Logistics and are approved in writing.

## 43. Social media
43.1 Do not post photos or videos of customer cargo, documents, vehicle plates or the warehouse interior without permission from the managing director.
43.2 When you talk about the company on your personal accounts, make clear that these are your own opinions.
43.3 Do not respond to complaints from customers on social media. Pass them to customer service.

## 44. Employee referral bonus
44.1 If you recommend a candidate who is hired and passes probation, you receive a referral bonus of 30,000 ALL for warehouse and driver roles, and 50,000 ALL for office and specialist roles.
44.2 The bonus is paid with the salary after the new colleague completes probation. HR employees and the hiring manager cannot receive it.
44.3 Send the candidate's CV to hr@drini-logistics.example and mention your name.
